## Risk Analysis

1. **Risk:** On macOS with Homebrew Miniforge cask installed, `/opt/homebrew/bin/conda` (a symlink to
   `Caskroom/miniforge/base/condabin/conda`) is on `$PATH` as a raw binary after `brew shellenv`, which previously
   caused `if not command -q conda` to skip Conda initialization without activating `base` or adding `base/bin` to
   `$PATH`.
   - **Mitigation:** `00-devenv.fish` probes candidate Conda roots and unconditionally pipes
     `"$conda_root/bin/conda" shell.fish hook 2>/dev/null | source` on the first existing root, regardless of whether a
     `conda` binary is already on `$PATH`, and `tests/test_shell_hooks.py` explicitly tests this exact symlink-masking
     layout under `env -i`.
2. **Risk:** Conda's `shell.<sh> hook` prepends `<conda_root>/bin` to `$PATH` when activating `base`, which pushes an
   earlier `$NVM_DIR/current/bin` entry down in `$PATH` and lets a Conda-installed `node` shadow NVM's `node`.
   - **Mitigation:** `00-devenv.fish`, `.bashrc`, and `.zshrc` all re-assert NVM `current/bin` and `~/.local/bin` at the
     front of `$PATH` _after_ the Conda/Micromamba hook block.
